Our Journey

2010

The Journey Begins

Baba arrived in Taiwan with a dream — to share the soul of Indian cuisine with a new land. From humble beginnings, he carried generations of culinary heritage and a heart full of passion.

2021

The First Baba Indian Restaurant

Baba opened the very first Baba Indian Restaurant at 198 Minzu Road. What started as a small dream quickly became a local favorite from day one, as guests embraced authentic Indian flavors for the first time.

Our first home was at 198 Minzu Road.
2024

Wuri Welcomes Baba

The second branch opened in Wuri, where local residents welcomed foreign cuisine with warm smiles and open hearts. A new chapter began, connecting cultures through food.

2025

A Bigger Home for Bigger Love

We moved from 198 Minzu Road to a bigger space at 189 Minzu Road to welcome more guests. Our new home was created to serve comfort, connection, and unforgettable dining moments — and the love from our community made it all worthwhile.

2026

Serving Thousands, Creating Memories

By 2026, Baba proudly served over 10,000+ happy guests, turning meals into memories and strangers into family. Every dish carries gratitude, every table tells a story.

More than a restaurant — a place where cultures meet, stories are shared, and every guest becomes family.
