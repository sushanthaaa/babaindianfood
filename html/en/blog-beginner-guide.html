A Beginner's Guide to Indian Food in Taichung: What to Order & Spice Levels

Stepping into an Indian restaurant for the first time can be slightly overwhelming with so many unfamiliar names and vibrant descriptions. Don't worry! Baba Indian Restaurant has prepared this simple guide to help you start your culinary journey in Taichung like a pro.

Indian cuisine is much more than just "curry"; it's an art form centered on the balance of spices, texture, and temperature. Each dish has its own unique heritage and preparation technique. Here are our recommended classic combinations for beginners:

1. Choose Your Core: Meat or Vegetarian?

In India, vegetarian culture is incredibly sophisticated. If you're a meat lover, **Butter Chicken** is an absolute must-try. Its creamy texture and fragrant aroma make it perfect for first-timers. For those exploring vegetarian options, **Paneer Masala** offers a hearty and satisfying experience with its firm cottage cheese cubes.

2. Bread and Rice: The Essential Companions

The essence of Indian dining is how you "scoop" the sauce. We highly recommend ordering handmade **Naan**. Plain Naan lets the curry's flavor shine, while Garlic Naan adds a delicious extra layer. If you prefer rice, the **Biryani**, made with fragrant long-grain Basmati rice, will surely impress you—every grain is infused with spice essence.

3. Understanding Spice Levels: How to Communicate Your Preference?

This is the most common question from our guests. At Baba, all our curries can be adjusted to your taste:

  • Non-Spicy: Perfect for children or those who avoid heat entirely. You'll get all the aroma without the sting.
  • Mild: Our most recommended level; it gently highlights the warmth of the spices.
  • Medium: For those who regularly enjoy spicy food; the heat is distinct and satisfying.
  • Spicy: A real challenge for the adventurous souls who love a serious kick.

Finally, don't forget to order a **Mango Lassi** to finish. This yogurt-based drink is not only refreshing but also perfect for balancing the spices on your palate.

Ready to start your feast?

Visit Baba Indian Restaurant today and experience the most authentic Indian flavors in Taichung.
